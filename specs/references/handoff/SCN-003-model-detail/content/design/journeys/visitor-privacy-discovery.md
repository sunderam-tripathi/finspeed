## Goal
Help visitors understand how Finspeed handles their information by making legal documents easy to discover and read.

## Key touchpoints
- Footer links to Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
- Cookie consent banner with "Learn more" link to privacy policy.
- Support contact page reiterating privacy contact information.

## Content sources
- `20-requirements/data/privacy-policy.md`
- `20-requirements/data/terms-of-use.md`
- `20-requirements/data/contact-points.yaml`

## Success signals
- Visitors spend >30 seconds on policy pages when they open them.
- No more than 5% bounce from consent banner "Learn more" link due to 404s or missing content.
