## 1. Introduction
Finspeed ("we", "our", "us") operates the website `https://finspeed.online` to showcase our cycle products and dealer network. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit our site, subscribe to updates, or contact our support team.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Contact information**: name, email address, and optional phone number when you submit support requests or subscribe to the blog.
- **Usage data**: page views, navigation actions, and click events (e.g., "Find a Dealer" directions, support email, WhatsApp). Analytics data is anonymized and collected only after consent where required.
- **Device data**: browser type, operating system, and anonymized IP address to help us monitor site performance and security.

## 3. How we use information
- Respond to your enquiries within our 6-hour SLA.
- Send blog updates when you opt in via the subscription form.
- Measure engagement to improve our site and product offerings.
- Ensure site performance, detect potential abuse, and protect against fraud.

## 4. Legal basis
- **Consent**: for marketing communications and optional analytics cookies.
- **Legitimate interest**: for basic usage analytics, site debugging, and responding to support requests.

## 5. Sharing and disclosure
We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating the site:
- **Formspree** for processing blog subscription submissions (stores email and message content).
- **Google Analytics 4** for anonymized site analytics.
All providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may only use it to deliver their services to us.

## 6. Data retention
- Support enquiries: retained for up to 24 months.
- Blog subscription data: retained until you unsubscribe or for a maximum of 24 months of inactivity.
- Analytics data: retained in aggregated form for 24 months.

## 7. Your rights
You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing `support@finspeed.online`. You may unsubscribe from blog updates at any time via the footer link or by contacting us.

## 8. Cookies and tracking
We use essential cookies for site functionality and optional analytics cookies. You can manage cookies through the consent banner and your browser settings. Blocking cookies may impact certain features.

## 9. Security
We use SSL encryption, access controls, and regular reviews to protect your data. While no method is 100% secure, we strive to use commercially acceptable safeguards.

## 10. International data transfers
Our services may store or process data in countries outside India, including the United States. By using our site, you consent to such transfers, subject to applicable data protection laws.

## 11. Updates to this policy
We may update this policy periodically. Updates will be indicated by the "last reviewed" date. Material changes will be announced via our website.

## 12. Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact `support@finspeed.online` or WhatsApp `+91 96506 08982` during support hours (09:00–21:00 IST).
