## Why commuting by cycle still wins

Indian cities move fast, and traffic can feel unpredictable, yet cycling continues to be the most agile way to cover short and medium-distance commutes. A Finspeed bike combines durable frames with responsive handling, letting you thread through morning rush while keeping costs down. Safety starts with mindset: assume you are visible when you make yourself visible, and predictable when you follow clear routines.

## Pre-ride ritual (3 minutes)

**1. ABC quick check**  
Perform the classic Air, Brakes, Chain review. Inflate tyres to the recommended PSI range for your model (find it on the sidewall). Squeeze front and rear disc brakes to confirm bite and lever travel. Spin the chainring backward to ensure the drivetrain runs smooth and debris-free.

**2. Fit for visibility**  
Match your commute lighting to your environment. In early-morning or post-sunset rides, combine a 300-lumen front light with a flashing rear light. Clip reflective bands to your ankles—Finspeed crank-based motion draws attention faster than jacket strips alone.

**3. Adjust load**  
If you carry a laptop or groceries, balance weight centrally in a pannier or backpack. Keep heavy items low to maintain your centre of gravity, especially on our ATB and MTB frames.

## On-road techniques

**Hold a primary position**  
When road width narrows or you approach parked vehicles, ride a metre from the curb. This keeps you out of the door zone and convinces following vehicles to overtake only when it’s safe.

**Signal early, exit decisively**  
Urban traffic often misreads late hand signals. Indicate at least 30 metres before your turn, check over your shoulder, and take the lane as you steer. Our wide handlebars on the Shark series give you stability for confident manoeuvres.

**Scan for surface hazards**  
Watch for potholes, construction plates, and stray cables. The 27.5-inch wheels on Finspeed MTB models roll over irregularities, but spotting them early lets you steer smoothly instead of swerving.

## Gear that protects

- **Helmet**: Look for BIS-certified lids with MIPS or equivalent rotational protection. Adjust straps so only two fingers fit between chin and strap.
- **Gloves**: Padded palms absorb vibration on rough tarmac. They also protect skin if you need to brace during a slide.
- **Rain-ready layers**: A breathable shell keeps you dry without trapping sweat. Water-resistant shoe covers help maintain traction on pedals.
- **Anti-theft kit**: A hardened U-lock paired with a cable for wheels ensures your bike is waiting for the ride home.

## Route planning with technology

Use navigation apps that support cycling mode. Plot a route the night before and identify stretches with segregated lanes or lower average speeds. ## Post-ride wrap-up

Wipe down your drivetrain after rainy rides, especially if you rely on a daily indoor parking spot. Recharge lights immediately so they’re ready for the next day. Log any rattles or shifting delays—addressing them at a Finspeed dealer early prevents small issues from escalating.

## Build a community

Commuting is safer when riders support each other. Join local cycling groups or online forums where Finspeed owners share new infrastructure updates, safe detours, and pop-up events. Pair a weekly group ride with your commute to boost visibility and morale.
